VErification, Logic, and CRyptOgraphy

VeLCrO is a one-day workshop on computer science research spanning formal verification, logic, and cryptography. The day brings together researchers and students working in these areas for a program of talks and discussion.

The workshop will take place on December 11 at the Chennai Mathematical Institute, Chennai.
